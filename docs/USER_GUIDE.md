# Mahakavya User Guide

Welcome to **महाकाव्य** (Mahakavya) - where culture meets cutting-edge AI! This comprehensive guide will help you make the most of our AI-powered social platform.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Creating Your Account
1. Visit [mahakavya.social](https://mahakavya.social)
2. Click "Sign Up" and choose your preferred method:
   - Email and password
   - Google account
   - GitHub account
3. Complete your profile with cultural interests
4. Verify your email address

### Profile Setup
- **Profile Picture**: Upload a clear, friendly photo
- **Bio**: Share your cultural background and interests
- **Location**: Help others find local cultural events
- **Languages**: List languages you speak
- **Cultural Interests**: Select traditions, arts, and practices you love
